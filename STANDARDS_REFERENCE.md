# Tasmanian Subdivision Design Standards — Extracted Reference

Distilled compliance data for the generative subdivision layout app. Extracted 2026-06-10 from
the three reference PDFs in this folder so they don't need to be re-read. Full searchable text
extracts of each PDF are in `reference_extracts/*.txt` (with `===== PAGE n =====` markers);
rendered images of key standard drawing sheets are in `reference_extracts/*.png`.

Sources:
- **SPP** = State Planning Provisions, Tasmanian Planning Scheme (effective 24 Jan 2024)
- **TIG** = Tasmanian Infrastructure Guidelines, LGAT 2025
- **TSD** = Tasmanian Municipal Standard Drawings v3, Dec 2020 (vector drawings — text not extractable; R06/R07 read visually)

## 1. PLANNING SCHEME — LOT DESIGN STANDARDS (SPP)

### 1.1 General Residential Zone (GRZ) — clause 8.6 (PDF pp. 75–79) — PRIMARY TARGET ZONE

**8.6.1 A1 Lot design (Acceptable Solution):** each lot must
- Area ≥ **450 m²**, AND
- Contain a building envelope of **10 m × 15 m** at gradient **not steeper than 1:5**, clear of:
  - all setbacks per 8.4.2 A1–A3 and 8.5.1 A1–A2
  - easements/title restrictions
- (P1 performance path: "sufficient useable area" judged on topography, hazards, open space, local pattern — no fixed minimum)

**8.6.1 A2 Frontage:** ≥ **12 m** (excluding open space/utility/riparian lots).
P2 fallback: right-of-carriageway access allowed, min width **3.6 m**.

**8.6.1 A3 Access:** every lot must have vehicular access from lot boundary to a road, per road authority requirements.

**8.6.1 A4 Solar orientation:** lots in a subdivision with a new road must have long axis oriented
**within 30° east/west of true north** (i.e. long axis between N30°W and N30°E).
P4 fallback: orientation judged on merit.

**8.6.2 Roads:** A1 = "no new roads" (so any new road goes to performance criteria P1).
P1 factors the generator should address/report: council road network plan, road hierarchy,
connections to common boundaries of adjoining land (future subdivision), connectivity with
surrounding road/pedestrian/cycle/public-transport networks, minimised travel distances,
bicycle infrastructure on new collectors per Austroads AGRD06A, topography, balance lots.

**8.6.3 Services (Acceptable Solutions):** each lot must have:
- A1: connection to full water supply service
- A2: connection to reticulated sewerage (NO performance criterion — mandatory)
- A3: capable of connecting to public stormwater system (P3 fallback: on-site stormwater
  management judged on lot size, topography, soils, imperviousness, watercourses)

**8.4.2 Setbacks (GRZ dwellings) — feed into the 10×15 envelope test:**
- Primary frontage setback: ≥ **4.5 m** (garage/carport: ≥ **5.5 m**, or 1 m behind building line; 1 m if ground slopes >1:5 for 10 m from frontage)
- Non-primary (secondary/corner) frontage: ≥ **3 m**
- Building envelope: 45° planes from 3 m height at side boundaries; **4 m rear boundary** distance (internal lots: 4.5 m from rear boundary of property with adjoining frontage) — Figures 8.1–8.3
- Practical lot envelope test for generator: rectangle 10×15 m must fit clear of 4.5 m front,
  3 m secondary-street side, and ~4 m rear setbacks, on ground ≤ 20% slope.

### 1.3 Other SPP triggers to remember
- Bushfire-Prone Areas Code **C13.0** (Table C13.1): through roads in bushfire areas min carriageway **7.0 m**; cul-de-sac >200 m needs 7 m carriageway; cul-de-sac head min turning radius **12.0 m**; 4.0 m vertical clearance on accesses.
- Natural Assets Code **C7.0**: works near watercourses/protected waterways.
- Habitable buildings ≥ **300 mm above 1% AEP flood level** (Building Act).

## 2. ROAD DESIGN (TIG §3 + TSD R06/R07)

### 2.1 Urban road hierarchy — PREFERRED widths (TIG Table 1, p.16–17; carriageway = full width to face of kerb)

| Road type | Length/tenements | Road width | Reserve width | Footpaths |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arterial | any | 14.3 m | 25.0 m | 3.0 m one side + 1.8 m other |
| Sub-arterial | any | 11.0 m | 24.0 m | 3.0 m + 1.8 m |
| Collector | any | 11.0 m | 22.0 m | 3.0 m + 1.8 m |
| Local (through) | any | 8.9 m | 20.0 m | 1.8 m both sides |
| Local cul-de-sac > 150 m | — | 8.9 m | 20.0 m | 1.8 m both sides |
| Local cul-de-sac ≤ 150 m | — | 6.9 m | 17.0 m | 1.8 m one side |

### 2.2 Urban road hierarchy — MINIMUM widths (TIG Table 4, p.18, from TSD R06)

| Road type | Road width | Reserve width | Footpaths |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arterial | 14.3 m | 25.0 m | 1.5 m both sides |
| Sub-arterial | 11.0 m | 20.0 m | 1.5 m both sides |
| Collector | 11.0 m | 20.0 m | 1.5 m both sides |
| Local (through) | 8.9 m | 18.0 m | 1.5 m one side |
| Local cul-de-sac > 150 m | 8.9 m | 18.0 m | 1.5 m one side |
| Local cul-de-sac ≤ 150 m | 6.9 m | 15.0 m | 1.5 m one side |

- **Intermediate widths NOT permitted:** between 6.9–8.9 m and between 8.9–11.0 m.
- 1.8 m+ shared paths both sides recommended within 400 m walk of open space/transit/business zones.
- Footpath set back ≥ **100 mm** from property boundary (TSD R06). Min footpath width 1.5 m (1.2 m at obstacles); cross fall 2% to kerb; 1.8 m may be required at cul-de-sac heads.

### 2.3 Longitudinal gradients (TIG 3.4.5, p.22)
- Minimum grade: **0.5 %** (also kerb & channel min 0.5%)
- Maximum: **< 17 % for ≤ 200 m** (up to 20 % for < 70 m only with GM/CEO approval)
- **< 10 %** for bus/heavy-vehicle routes
- Intersections: grade must achieve Safe Intersection Sight Distance (TSD RF01)

### 2.4 Cross falls (TIG 3.4.6)
- Sealed roads 3.0–5.0 % (urban and rural); turning heads 3–5 %; unsealed 4–6 %

### 2.5 Intersections & curves (TIG 3.4.7, Table 12)
- Design vehicle local/local (residential): **8.8 m service vehicle, 9 m turn radius** (12 m outer radius in bushfire areas)
- Collector/collector or arterial/collector (residential): 12.5 m single-unit truck/bus, radius 12.5 m
- Arterial/arterial: 19 m semi-trailer, radius 15 m
- Minimum kerb radius at local-road intersections: **5 m**
- All roads must allow safe turning for 5.2 m passenger car; service vehicle (8.8 m) where waste collection
- Preferred: 10 m radius turning circle for fire trucks without reversing

### 2.6 Cul-de-sac turning heads (TSD R07, drawing read visually)
- Circular head: **18.0 m diameter face-of-kerb**, kerb transition radius **20 m (typ.)**
- Reserve at head: **25 m** wide; min **2.0 m clearance** kerb-to-reserve-boundary (services, overhang, pedestrians)
- Accommodates: single-movement turn by 12.0 m-circle design car; 3-point turn by 8.8 m service vehicle
- Offset-circular and tee variants exist (tee ≈ 24.5 m × 18.0 m envelope); tees/wyes discouraged
- Temporary cul-de-sacs must allow garbage-truck manoeuvring

### 2.7 Rural roads (TIG Table 6, p.19 — for rural-living style subdivisions)
- S3 (100–300 AADT): 5.5 m dual traffic width, 6.5 m carriageway
- S4 (300–2000): 6.0 m dual, 7.0 m carriageway; S5 (>2000): 7.0 m dual, 9.0 m carriageway
- Bushfire-prone: min 7 m carriageway for through roads

### 2.8 Embankments / batters (TIG 3.9.2)
- Max batter slopes per TSD R06 Table 3 by soil type, up to **1V:3H in sand** (loose rock 1:0.25, solid rock steeper; clay ~1:2 cut / 1:2.5 fill — confirm per TSD R06 Table 3)
- Austroads: max 1V:4H preferred for mowing/maintenance; up to 2V:1H only with geotech assessment
- Fill > 300 mm requires AS3798 certification
- Wider reserves needed on steep cross-slopes to contain batters

### 2.9 Driveways (TIG 3.7)
- Width typically **3.6 m** (to 7.2 m with approval)
- Separations (preferred): ≥1.0 m from side boundaries, ≥6.0 m from intersection tangent points, ≥7.0 m from other crossings, ≥2.0 m from poles/kerb ramps
- Every lot must demonstrate achievable driveway access on engineering drawings

### 2.10 Typical urban cross-section (TSD R06, visual)
Property bdy → nature strip → footpath (100 mm off bdy) → face of kerb → carriageway (3–5 % crossfall) → kerb → nature strip/footpath → property bdy.
Pavement (min): 35 mm asphalt AC10 + 165 mm Class A base + 135 mm sub-base (or 200 mm base + two-coat spray seal).

## 3. SERVICES IN ROAD RESERVE (TIG 3.14)

- All new electrical underground; TasNetworks designs/reviews; kiosk substations ≥3 m from driveways, ≥1.6 m from back of footpath
- Underground services within road reserve, **preferably NOT under carriageway or parking** — use nature strip / under footpaths
- Water & sewer mains: **not within 1 m of property boundaries**; need **3 m wide maintenance corridor** clear of trees/poles
- Water mains on steep cross-slope: install on the **uphill (cut) side** of road
- Water/sewer design per TasWater supplements to WSA03 (water) and WSA02 (gravity sewer) — *not in the reference folder; concept layouts should flag "per TasWater WSA02/03"*

## 4. STORMWATER (TIG §4)

### 4.1 System framework
- Gravity discharge to council system via single connection wherever practicable
- **Minor system** (pipes/pits): design AEP — residential **preferred 5 %, minimum 10 %**; rural >2 ha 10–20 %; commercial/industrial/CBD 2–5 %
- **Major system** (overland flow paths): convey **1 % AEP + climate change (SSP5-8.5, year 2100)** without undue inundation of private property; overland flow via roads/public land preferred, easements discouraged
- Stormwater Management Report required for residential development > **2500 m²** (so: always, for this app)

### 4.2 Flood safety on overland flow paths (1 % AEP + CC)
- Preferred H1: depth ≤ 0.3 m, velocity ≤ 2.0 m/s
- Minimum: depth × velocity < **0.35**, depth < 0.5 m, velocity < 3 m/s

### 4.3 Hydrology
- Rational Method OK for simple catchments < 1 ha; larger needs IL-CL/RORB/RAFTS modelling
- Fraction impervious (Table 16): urban residential low density (5–20 dw/ha incl. roads) **0.45–0.85**; townhouses 0.7; roads/paving 0.9; open space 0
- Climate change factors from ARR Data Hub; minimum scenario SSP3-7.0 (2100)

### 4.4 Pipe network design minimums (TIG 4.6)
- Min pipe velocity 0.75 m/s (depth > D/2) or 1.0 m/s (depth < D/2); max 5.0 m/s
- Min pipe grade **0.4 %**; anchor blocks at grades ≥ 10 %
- Min road drainage pipe diameter **DN300**; property connections **DN150**
- Pits at each low point + tangent points; max **90 m of kerb** draining to one entry pit; entry pit depth ≤ 1.5 m
- Manholes at ends, junctions, changes of direction/grade; max spacing **100 m** (< DN1200)
- Pipes located in road reserves preferred; through private property → along lot boundaries, grouped with sewer

### 4.5 Detention & quality
- Any runoff increase must be contained in existing/upgraded system or detention; subdivision scale → engineered detention/retention basin with site-specific modelling
- Lot-scale defaults: <200 m² extra impervious → 500 L OSD in ≥2000 L tank, 25 mm orifice; 200–300 m² → 1000 L in ≥4000 L; >300 m² → engineered design
- Quality (developments creating ≥500 m² impervious, subdivisions >5 lots): reduce annual loads — gross pollutants 90 %, TSS 80 %, TP 45 %, TN 45 % (minimum standard: 90 % litter + 80 % TSS)
- Bioretention swale sized ~**1 % of catchment area** generally achieves targets (useful sizing rule for auto-placing a WSUD/detention reserve)
